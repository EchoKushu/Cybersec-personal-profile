02 / 06 · Customer appointments

A calendar for the working day

Month, week and day views let the team switch between the wider schedule and the detail of a single day.

Calendar navigation

Move between dates or return to today. Add Booking opens the appointment form.

Weekly statistics

The calendar includes therapist hours, earnings and appointment totals.

Booking management

The interface exposes booking details and deletion controls. The site instructions also describe editing existing bookings; no records were changed during this review.

03 / 06 · Appointment capture

The details a booking needs

A single form gathers the customer, timing, staff assignment and payment status.

Customer & time

Customer name, phone number, appointment date and time, and duration in minutes are required fields.

Staff & location

Assign a staff member and record the appointment location.

Payment status

Choose Paid, Not Paid or Groupon Voucher, with an optional voucher code. This records a status; a payment-processing integration was not verified.

04 / 06 · People & availability

See who can work, and when

Staff management and a monthly availability planner provide the foundation for scheduling.

Staff profiles

Add a name, email address and phone number. The staff list provides edit and activate/deactivate controls and displays a pay-rate column.

Monthly planner

Choose a month to view availability in a date-by-staff grid.

Availability updates

Each staff column provides an Edit Month link for maintaining availability. Personal staff details are omitted from this presentation.

05 / 06 · Clinic rota

Turn availability into a weekly plan

The weekly rota brings shifts, appointment exceptions and coverage into view.

Plan the week

Previous, current and next-week navigation helps the team move through the rota. Add Shift and Copy Previous Week controls support planning.

Create a shift

The shift form includes staff, date, start and end times, notes, and an optional hourly-rate override.

Review coverage

Dedicated sections surface appointments needing attention, coverage and staffing gaps. The instructions describe overlap warnings and time suggestions; these were not tested by creating shifts.

06 / 06 · Access & payment records

Separate access for sensitive areas

The main login unlocks the calendar and staff screens. Payment details and the staff log display additional password gates.

Payment details

The Payments link opens an Unlock Payment Details screen. The site manual describes tracking staff payments and outstanding earnings; the underlying payment screens were not reviewed.

Staff log

Staff Log requires its own password. The manual describes recent staff login entries and summary statistics; those records were not accessed.

Review scope

The presentation is based on a signed-in, read-only review of the visible screens and the application instructions. Saving, editing, deleting, payment handling and scheduling validation were not exercised.
