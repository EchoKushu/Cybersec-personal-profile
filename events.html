Sustainable Futures Expo

The Sustainable Futures Expo is a developing exhibition and collaboration initiative bringing together organisations and innovators working across sustainability, environmental technology, responsible manufacturing and future-focused solutions.

Its purpose is to connect ideas, products, businesses and expertise, creating opportunities for commercial partnerships, knowledge exchange and practical innovation.

Details including dates and venues are still to be confirmed.

Exhibitors & businesses

Create a route to meaningful collaboration

The initiative will create opportunities for businesses to exhibit products and services, manufacturers to present innovations, technology companies to demonstrate solutions and researchers to share developments.

International exhibitors may also explore UK opportunities, while sustainable suppliers develop commercial relationships.
